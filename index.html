Qualification, Validation & Pilot Build

NPI
Objective
Prove process capability and product quality before commercial launch.
Key Activities

Deliverables
Approved PPAP/qualification dossier, mass production pricing activation, ramp readiness assessment.
NPI → Production Transfer Criteria
Before a part or supplier moves out of NPI into sustaining production, I hold it to explicit transfer criteria: Commercial — final negotiated volume-tier pricing active in ERP, executed MSA and QAA on file. Quality — 100% completed FAI/PPAP approval, zero open critical issues. Supply Chain — safety stock/consignment agreement established, lead times locked, ERP master data complete.
